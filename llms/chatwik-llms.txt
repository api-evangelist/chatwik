# Chatwik

> Chatwik is the modern customer communication platform. Connect all your support channels, collaborate with team members, and drive business growth through unified conversations and AI automation.

## Core Products & Platforms
- [Shared Inbox](https://chatwik.com/products/shared-inbox): Centralized omni-channel inbox for managing customer conversations across email, live chat, social media, and messaging apps.
- [CRM Workspace](https://chatwik.com/products/crm): Integrated customer relationship management system with conversation histories, contacts, custom attributes, and automated tagging.
- [WikAI Assistant](https://chatwik.com/products/wikai): Autonomous AI support agent trained on your knowledge base to resolve customer queries instantly 24/7.

## Supported Communication Channels
- [Website Live Chat](https://chatwik.com/features/website): Customizable website widget with embeddable JS SDK.
- [WhatsApp Inbox](https://chatwik.com/features/whatsapp): Official WhatsApp Business API integration for multi-agent support.
- [Facebook Messenger](https://chatwik.com/features/facebook): Direct message routing and automation for Facebook Pages.
- [Instagram DMs](https://chatwik.com/features/instagram): Unified messaging for Instagram Direct Messages.
- [Telegram Bot](https://chatwik.com/features/telegram): Automated & human-assisted support via Telegram bots.
- [SMS Messaging](https://chatwik.com/features/sms): Two-way customer texting and notification dispatch.
- [Voice Support](https://chatwik.com/features/voice): Cloud phone system and AI voice call routing.
- [Email Desk](https://chatwik.com/features/email): Turn support emails into actionable collaborative tickets.
- [Line Channel](https://chatwik.com/features/line): Multi-agent support for Line Official Accounts.
- [TikTok & X (Twitter)](https://chatwik.com/features/tiktok): Social support integration for modern brands.
